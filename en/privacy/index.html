Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Teklif does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads or analytics. What you enter is stored only on your phone and never reaches us.

What the app stores

The following information you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your business details: name, phone, address and the logo you choose
  • Your customers: name and an optional phone number
  • Quotes, line items, payments and document numbers

None of this is sent to us or to third parties. Deleting the app removes it from your phone.

Photo access

When you add a logo, the app only accesses the image you pick. It is resized on your phone and kept inside the app so it can appear on your quotes.

Sharing and WhatsApp

Quotes, payment documents and backup files are shared only when you choose to, through your phone's share sheet, to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). The "Message customer" and "Remind" buttons open WhatsApp with a prefilled message; sending it is up to you. Those apps handle what you share under their own privacy policies.

Backups

"Save backup" writes all your data into a single file on your phone. You decide where to keep it; we never receive or see this file. Because it contains customer details, keep it somewhere you trust.

Your customers' information

You are responsible for the customer information you enter. Use it only for your own business and in line with the data protection laws that apply to you.

Children

Teklif is designed for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the changes on this page with a new date.
