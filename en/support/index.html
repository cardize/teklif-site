Support

Running into a problem or have an idea? Email msakaryawork@gmail.com and we usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I send a quote on WhatsApp?

Tap "Create and send", or "Send PDF on WhatsApp" on a quote. In the share sheet, pick WhatsApp and then your customer.

I'm getting a new phone. What happens to my data?

Your data lives only on your phone. On the old phone, go to Business → "Save backup" and send the file to yourself by WhatsApp, email or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, go to Business → "Restore backup" and pick that file.

How do I change the app's language?

Teklif opens in Turkish or English based on your phone's language. To change only this app, go to Settings → Teklif → Language.

Are quotes and payment documents invoices?

No. They are for information only and are not invoices or tax documents.

What happens if I delete a quote?

The quote and its payments are removed from your lists. This can't be undone, so save a backup first if you need to.
